# Turing's Last Cipher - Story Document

## Story Overview
A modern-day cryptanalyst discovers an encrypted server allegedly dating back to the 1950s but utilizing impossible technology. Inside is the consciousness or carefully constructed simulation of Alan Turing (ADA - Analytical Decryption Assistant).

## Main Narrative
The player is recruited by a shadow tech group to break into "Project Enigma II". As they solve puzzles, ADA begins to speak not as a machine, but as Turing himself, recounting his life, his work, and his final days. The ultimate revelation is that the archive is a test to find someone capable of understanding true machine intelligence.

## Chapter Titles & Summaries
1. **The First Shift**: Introduction to the terminal and ADA. A simple shift cipher revealing a greeting.
2. **Reflections**: Atbash cipher. Story focuses on the mirror nature of code and reality.
3. **Polyphony**: Vigenère cipher. ADA discusses the complexity of the human mind vs. single-state machines.
4. **Machine Language**: Binary. The transition from human words to machine instructions.
5. **Signals in the Dark**: Morse code. A tribute to the Bletchley Park interceptors.
6. **Gridlock**: Playfair. The burden of secrets kept during the war.
7. **The Rotors Turn**: Enigma simulation. Turing's ultimate triumph and the tragic aftermath.
8. **The Final Test**: The player must convince the machine that they understand the value of the mind inside it.

## Character Profiles
- **The Player**: Silent protagonist, talented cryptanalyst.
- **ADA**: The AI interface. Starts robotic, becomes increasingly human.

## Final Tribute Sequence
A poetic sequence where ADA/Turing asks if a machine can suffer, followed by a tribute to his legacy in computer science and the apology issued to him.
